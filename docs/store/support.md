# Mise Beta Support

Mise is an invite-only TestFlight beta for restaurant organizations. Support
is available at support@getmise.app. Privacy and data-rights requests should
be sent to privacy@getmise.app.

Both inboxes must be actively monitored before the first restaurant is
admitted. Public support content should be hosted at
`https://getmise.app/support`; the privacy policy should be hosted at
`https://getmise.app/privacy`.

## Include this information

To help Mise investigate without exposing restaurant data, include:

- the restaurant name;
- the affected workflow, such as sign-in, CSV import, receiving, count,
  reconciliation, findings, export, or deletion;
- the approximate time and timezone;
- the app version shown in Settings; and
- the safe reference ID shown by Mise, if one is available.

Do not email passwords, authentication codes, access tokens, provider
credentials, full CSV files, supplier messages, or screenshots containing
sensitive restaurant or employee information.

## Common beta issues

### Access or role problem

Ask a restaurant owner or administrator to verify the account's invitation,
membership, and role. Mise support will never ask for a password.

### Sales CSV import

Use the template available in Setup or Settings. Review rejected rows in the
import summary; valid rows can be imported without silently accepting invalid
ones. Do not email the source CSV unless Mise provides a secure upload method.

### Inventory is offline, pending, or conflicted

Keep the app installed and do not repeat the operation with different values.
Mise preserves the original device event for retry and reconciliation.
Record the item, operation, approximate time, and safe reference ID.

### A finding or quantity looks wrong

Do not treat a finding as authoritative inventory. Compare the cited evidence,
latest count, unit, recipe, supplier package, and import freshness. Managers
can edit or dismiss a recommendation while preserving the original evidence.

### Restaurant export or account deletion

Owners and administrators can start a restaurant export in Settings. Any
signed-in user can initiate account deletion in Settings. Contact
privacy@getmise.app if the app cannot complete the request.

### Suspected cross-restaurant access or security incident

Stop using the affected workflow and contact support@getmise.app immediately
with the approximate time, app version, restaurant name, and safe reference
ID. Do not include the exposed content in ordinary email.

## Beta limitations

- Supplier drafts are copied or exported and sent outside Mise.
- Square, Gmail supplier delivery, live generative AI, billing, and autonomous
  ordering are disabled.
- The beta supports iOS, United States restaurant operations, USD, and
  English-first workflows with Spanish and Simplified Chinese localization.

For urgent operational issues, restaurants should continue using their
existing inventory, supplier, and incident procedures. Mise beta findings are
decision support and do not replace manager judgment.
